Six directions for The Dispatch

Same content, six different treatments. Each tile is the live page, not a screenshot: scroll inside it, hover things, resize it. Vote keep or kill, and we port the winners into the real repo.

01 - Untitled SaaS

Clean product UI: neutral gray, tight cards, restrained accents. The "probably safe" pick.

02 - Editorial

Serif headlines, wide margins, one accent. Reads like a journal, not a template.

03 - Terminal Night

Dark transit board: mono type, line colors, live-feed styling. Bridges the Atlas mood.

04 - Loud Chicago

Flag colors, oversized type, real motion. The "identity loud" direction.

05 - Atlas console

Dark HUD for the terminal and station panels, sampled beside a mini map.

06 - Interaction sheet

Individual Uiverse-style parts: buttons, checkboxes, loaders, tooltips. Mix and match, no page commitment.
